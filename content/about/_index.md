## 👋 Chi sono

With over **20 years of experience** in software engineering and education, I bridge the gap between **theory and practice**. I am a **Computer Science Teacher** and a **Lead Software Engineer**, currently working at **ELCA Group**.

I hold degrees in **Computer Engineering** and **Clinical Engineering**, and I've collaborated with organizations including **Objectway**, **Reply**, **Exprivia**, **Engineering**, and **ELCA** across **European R&D**, **financial accounting**, **healthcare**, **gambling**, and **energy**.

My professional passion lies in **teaching**, **training**, and **programming** — turning complex concepts into practical, hands-on knowledge.
