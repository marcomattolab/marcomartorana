# 🎓 AWS AI Practitioner: a "Who Wants to Be a Millionaire"-style quiz for AIF-C01

Studying for the **AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01)** exam usually means endless flashcards, dry documentation, and coffee. What if it felt more like sitting in the hot seat opposite Jerry Scotti, climbing from €500 to €1,000,000 — one question at a time?

That's exactly the idea behind the quiz app I built: **AWS AIF-C01 Exam Trainer**, a *"Who Wants to Be a Millionaire"* (the Italian *"Chi vuol essere milionario"*) style game that turns real exam questions into a proper show.

---

## 🎮 The concept

Same suspense, same ladder of prizes, same *"is that your final answer?"* tension — but every question is an actual AWS AI question.

- 🔢 **472 real exam questions** covering the full AIF-C01 domain
- 🪜 **Prize ladder** that mimics the TV show format
- 🛟 **Lifelines** to keep the game moving when you're stuck
- 🧮 **Scaled scoring**, not just right/wrong

Instead of memorizing answers, you *play* your way through the syllabus.

---

## 🧠 What it actually covers

The question bank mirrors the real AWS Certified AI Practitioner exam domains:

| Domain | What you'll practice |
|---|---|
| **Fundamentals of AI & ML** | Core concepts, terminology, model types |
| **Generative AI & Foundation Models** | LLMs, prompt engineering, Amazon Bedrock |
| **AWS AI/ML Services** | SageMaker, Rekognition, Comprehend, Polly, Transcribe |
| **Responsible AI** | Bias, fairness, transparency, governance |

Every session feels like a mini game show, and every mistake becomes a learning moment.

---

## 🛟 Lifelines, but make them educational

Just like on TV, when you're stuck you can:

- 📞 **50:50** — remove two wrong answers
- 👥 **Ask the audience** — see the community vote distribution
- 💬 **Community discussions** — read how others reasoned about the same question

The vote distributions and discussions turn a solo grind into something collaborative.
